Guildkeep
Search and admin for group chat

The name

Guildkeep

guild — a group of people who share a craft. keep — the fortified core of a castle; and the verb: to keep order, to keep the archive. The product does both: it keeps the group's history searchable and keeps its rules enforced. Two syllables, spelled the way it sounds, no hyphen.

Why it works
  • Abstract enough to cover both features (search + admin)
  • No "chat", "wa", "mod" or "bot" — nothing to defend against
  • Short, pronounceable in any language, types cleanly
  • Reads as a place, so "in Guildkeep" sounds natural
Checked and rejected
  • Reeve — several live products
  • Quorum — three software companies
  • Warden / Steward / Marshal — crowded moderation space
  • Guildhall — generic, event-venue software

The mark

A chat bubble wearing three battlements, with a magnifier punched out of it — keep + conversation + search. Two shapes and one cut-out, so it survives 16 px.

Nothing is hand-coloured. Every hue lives in PALETTE in src/brand.mjs; the build writes src/palette.css and both stylesheets only use variables. The logo, icons, OG card and these swatches read the same object.
